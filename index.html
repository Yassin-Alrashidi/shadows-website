FAQ

Questions we hear often

How do we start?

Message us on WhatsApp, by email or with the form below, with a few lines about your project. We reply within one working day and set up a short brief.

How is pricing set?

Pricing depends on the scope: what you need, how much and how often. After the brief we send a clear quote before any work begins.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A set of photos or a short video is quicker than a full brand identity or a monthly content plan. We agree the timeline with you before we start.

What is it like to work with us?

You get one point of contact and a clear plan. We share work in progress before anything goes live, take your notes and adjust. After launch we review the results and plan the next step.

Working terms

Every project follows the Shadows principles: no music in our content, and no women who do not wear the hijab on screen. We build creative alternatives that keep the quality and the marketing impact high. Scope, price and timeline are agreed with you before we start.

Do you work in Arabic and English?

Yes. We create and manage content in both languages.
